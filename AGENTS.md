# RESET publication rules

Before publishing a new case, article, service, or language version, read `docs/seo-editorial-release.md`. This policy applies to future releases; do not infer a Google spam penalty from a Search Console status alone.

Keep existing canonical URLs stable unless the user explicitly requests a measured URL migration. For such a migration, verify the old URL is not in the indexed baseline, improve the page for visitors, map the old URL to the new one with a direct permanent redirect, update internal links and metadata, and test the result. Do not change publication dates or apply mass `noindex` to create the appearance of a gradual launch. A translated case may describe exactly the same project in every language; translate the complete main content and maintain correct self-canonical and reciprocal `hreflang`.

The owner approved a limited staged rollout in `content/seo-release-queue.json`; it is not a general weekly schedule or an indexing guarantee. Other existing pages are already live and known to Google. The sitemap is temporarily limited to the measured cohort in `content/seo-sitemap-cohort.json`; this does not hide other live pages from crawlers. Do not temporarily remove existing valid pages or gate other URLs by date to imitate a fresh launch. Review indexing problems per URL and improve real crawl and content issues. Future genuinely new content must include verifiable project facts or first-hand analysis, accurate claims, and a useful purpose for visitors; keep drafts out of navigation and the sitemap until publication. Use `docs/seo-editorial-release.md` for the current indexing workflow.

Visible copy follows the owner's editorial rules in every locale: use a hyphen instead of an en dash or em dash; omit a final period in headings and in standalone one-sentence body blocks; retain normal punctuation when a paragraph has multiple sentences. Do not alter URL syntax, decimal numbers, code, structured data, or metadata to enforce this visual style. Simplify only mildly bureaucratic phrasing, without changing facts or replacing precise terms such as Meta Ads. After editing generated content, rebuild it and run `npm run build:ui` and `npm test`; the editorial test must pass and a second UI build must change zero pages.
