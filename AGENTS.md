# RESET publication rules

Before publishing a new case, article, service, or language version, read `docs/seo-editorial-release.md`. This policy applies to future releases; do not infer a Google spam penalty from a Search Console status alone.

Keep existing canonical URLs stable. Do not rename URLs, resubmit unchanged sitemaps, change publication dates, or apply mass `noindex` to create the appearance of a gradual launch. A translated case may describe exactly the same project in every language; translate the complete main content and maintain correct self-canonical and reciprocal `hreflang`.

Publish at most one reviewed content unit per weekly editorial release. A content unit can have four language URLs when all four translations have been reviewed. If a unit is not ready, skip the release rather than publish filler. Content must include verifiable project facts or first-hand analysis, accurate claims, and a useful purpose for visitors. Keep drafts out of navigation and the sitemap until publication. Use `docs/seo-editorial-release.md` for release and follow-up checks.
