RESET / CASE STUDY

A 57-hectare residential masterplan in Bulgaria.

For a mountain site near Kalofer, we developed a residential concept covering homes, roads, cycling routes, parking, public space and recreation, then assembled the masterplan and its visual presentation for the client.

RESET / APPROACH

A clear site structure for the next stage of development.

01

Read the terrain

We placed development and routes around the elevation change, existing landscape and forest setting.

02

Built the zoning

Residential clusters connect to public, sport, recreation and service areas.

03

Prepared the presentation

The planning scheme became an annotated masterplan and a visual concept for client review.

PRODUCT / WORKFLOW

From site geometry to a landscape concept.

01 / TERRAIN

The site ranges from 774 to 884 metres in elevation. The layout works with the slope and retains most of the land as green space.

02 / USES

The concept includes homes, parking, cycling, sport, public space, recreation and supporting services.

03 / CONNECTIONS

Roads, the entrance, pedestrian links and cycle routes form one movement system across the site.

The client received one coherent site concept with zoning, area balance, routes and a visual model ready for the next design discussion.
