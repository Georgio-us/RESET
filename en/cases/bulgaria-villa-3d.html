KALOFER VILLA3D architectureKalofer / Bulgaria

RESET / CASE STUDY

From floor plans to a 3D villa model in Bulgaria.

For a villa within the Kalofer development, we created floor plans, room schedules and a spatial model. This foundation supports architectural development, design checks and photorealistic material for presentations and marketing.

244 m²combined external area of two above-ground floors
2 floorsplans with room functions and areas
4 viewschecks of façades, volumes and internal connections

RESET / APPROACH

One model connects planning, architecture and presentation.

01

Developed the plans

We defined rooms, areas, stairs, terraces and the connections between levels.

02

Built the volume

The plans were translated into 3D to check proportions, glazing, façades, canopies and outdoor space.

03

Prepared for rendering

The model became a base for further design work and marketing material for the villas.

PRODUCT / WORKFLOW

The floor plans provide an exact base for the model.

01 / FUNCTIONS

Room schedules establish the purpose and area of every space before modelling begins.

02 / CONNECTIONS

A shared stair connects the levels, while openings remain coordinated across plans and façades.

03 / SCALE

Dimensions and areas keep the villa’s proportions controlled through each following stage.

The project gained a coordinated spatial base for refining the architecture, preparing working material and producing final presentation renders.
