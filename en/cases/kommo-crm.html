SHEPIT / KOMMO CRM

Leads in the CRM. Alerts in Telegram.

We configured Kommo for SHEPIT’s sales team: connected enquiry channels, set up the pipeline and built a Telegram bot. Managers track deals in the CRM and receive new-lead alerts in their team chat.

SHEPIT / SALES WORKFLOW

  1. 01Facebook and website
  2. 02Deal in Kommo
  3. 03Team notification

The task: connect marketing with the sales workflow

The project already had acquisition channels. The team needed a process for handling enquiries: collect leads in the CRM, track sales stages and notice new enquiries without constantly checking the system.

01

Kommo for the team’s workflow

We selected the CRM to fit SHEPIT’s processes and configured a pipeline for incoming enquiries.

02

Channels and integrations

We connected the required integrations so the team could handle enquiries from its channels in one system.

03

Notification bot

We built a Telegram bot that sends new-lead notifications to the sales team’s working group.

01 / KOMMO CRM

A pipeline that reflects the sales process

The screenshot shows stages from an unanswered call to first contact, a scheduled meeting and a completed meeting. Each card identifies the enquiry source; its stage shows where the deal stands.

02 / KOMMO

Enquiry channels connected to the CRM

The working account has Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp Business and TikTok installed. The screenshot shows these integrations; the Telegram notification bot operates separately from the Telegram integration in the catalogue.

Installed Kommo integrations in the project account.

03 / TELEGRAM

New leads are visible outside the CRM too

The bot sends the team a notification containing the enquiry source, contact details and submission time. These examples show enquiries from Facebook and the SHEPIT website. The team can notice incoming leads without keeping the CRM open in front of them.

The result: a working process for the sales team

SHEPIT’s team uses the configured CRM: enquiries enter the system, deals move through defined stages and new-lead alerts arrive in Telegram.
