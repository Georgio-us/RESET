SEO for a real estate agency in Spain

The agency came to us after losing organic traffic following a website migration. Since May 2026, we have been restoring reliable measurement and working on technical SEO. Organic search now brings enquiries that can be tracked in analytics.

01 / CONTEXT

From a migration to a measurable channel

The site migrated in December 2025. In May 2026, the agency brought in RESET to recover account access, investigate organic traffic and establish enquiry tracking. We connected Search Console, analytics and event tracking into a working measurement setup, so the team could assess enquiries as well as visits.

01 / ACCESS

Recovered working access

Restored the ability to manage analytics and review the website in Google Search Console.

02 / MEASUREMENT

Set up analytics and events

Organic sessions and lead events can be viewed separately from other channels.

03 / SEO

Investigated the migration aftermath

We review crawling, indexing, sitemaps and page routes, prioritising fixes for commercial sections.

05 / NEXT

Work continues

The next stage covers updating sitemaps, addressing technical URLs and missing resources, and reviewing commercial pages outside the index. September clicks were lower than in the equivalent August period; we continue to investigate changes by page and query.
