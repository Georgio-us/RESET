FACTOR / SEO · 2026

From site migration to a structured SEO launch

Since 17 August 2026, we have supported FACTOR’s SEO: a website migration following a redesign, keyword research, landing-page structure and content guidelines. Working alongside the technical team, we built a foundation for further organic growth.

01 / GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE

Early results in Google Search

The Search Console chart shows a rise in impressions and clicks in September. By 29 September, Google recorded a milestone of 1.2K clicks over the last 28 days.

Full-chart totals: 1.98K clicks, 50.9K impressions, 3.9% CTR and an average position of 10.6 across the selected period. These totals are not limited to our engagement. Clicks are not leads.

What we delivered

01

Migration support

Alongside the technical team, we checked the transfer of SEO pages following the redesign and monitored their availability and indexing.

02

Keyword research and launch priorities

Using Google Keyword Planner, we researched demand and organised queries by property category and buyer intent. This informed the page matrix and launch priorities.

03

Landing pages and blog

We launched 112 pages, worked on the blog and developed content guidelines covering relevant listings, up-to-date data and answers to buyers’ questions.

02 / KEYWORD PLANNER

Demand first Structure next

Of 2,689 search phrases, we retained 2,425 for further clustering. The first pass excluded 264 rental, branded, navigational and seller queries. We grouped demand into apartments, houses, new developments, land and other categories.

03 / FAQ

Content that helps buyers decide

We developed a three-question FAQ approach for tag pages: pricing and available listings, budget and price per m², and a specific buyer concern. Answers must reflect current listings and the relevant property segment and location.

Continuing to build organic visibility

Ukraine is the current priority. Work on international property and the blog continues. These are early results from migration and content development, not the outcome of years of SEO.
