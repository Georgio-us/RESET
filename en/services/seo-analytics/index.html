Before we start

When will the website appear in Google?

We can remove technical barriers and submit pages for processing. Search engines decide whether and when to index them.

Is installing analytics enough?

No. We need to define useful events, verify their delivery and distinguish visits from actual enquiries.

Do you guarantee top rankings?

No. We agree on concrete work and measurement, but do not promise positions controlled by search engines and affected by competition.
