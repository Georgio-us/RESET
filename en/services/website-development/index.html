Pricing and project scope

Cost is determined by page types and count, languages, catalogue features, content management and integrations. We prepare an estimate after agreeing on structure and materials.

Domain, hosting and paid services are listed separately. Post-launch support and content production are agreed as part of the scope.

Before we start

Can we use our existing website?

Yes. We first assess what can be kept and which changes would solve the task without rebuilding everything.

Who provides copy and photography?

At the start we agree which materials your team supplies and what needs to be produced within the project.

Is SEO included in development?

Technical preparation of the agreed pages is included. Ongoing SEO, keyword expansion and publishing content are separate work.
