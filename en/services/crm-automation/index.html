When it fits

When enquiries arrive through different channels, contact history is lost or managers lack a clear view of deals. We start with the team’s process, then choose the tools.

What the work includes

Scope agreed before work starts
01

Process map

We review lead sources, roles, deal stages and required follow-up actions.

02

Pipeline and data

We configure stages, fields, client records and property relationships in the chosen system.

03

Lead delivery

We connect agreed forms and sources, and check notifications, assignment and context retention.

04

Team launch

We test typical workflows, explain how the system works and set up agreed reports.

Before we start

Do we have to replace our CRM?

No. If the current system can meet the requirements, we work with its settings and integrations.

Can we migrate our existing database?

After checking structure and quality, we define what to migrate, how fields map and how to verify the result.

Will automation replace managers?

It helps transfer data, prompt actions and reduce manual work. Your team remains responsible for conversations and deals.
