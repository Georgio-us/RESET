Search advertising for real estate

We work with searches from people already looking for a property or service. We connect the search query, advertisement and landing page around a specific offer.

When it fits

For projects with relevant search demand and a website where visitors can explore the offer and enquire. We assess demand, geography and landing page readiness before launch.

What the work includes

Scope agreed before work starts
01

Demand and keywords

We research commercial queries, distinguish user intent and exclude irrelevant topics.

02

Campaign structure and ads

We group campaigns by offer and geography, and prepare ads and supporting assets.

03

Landing pages and events

We check page relevance, forms and enquiry measurement. Necessary website changes are scoped separately.

04

Search optimisation

We review search terms, costs and conversions, then adjust negative keywords, bids and budget allocation.

Before we start

Do we need a website?

Search ads need a suitable destination. We will review the existing page and identify whether changes are needed.

How do we assess performance?

Through agreed enquiry events and lead quality. Clicks and CTR help diagnose campaigns; they do not replace enquiries and sales.

What budget do we need?

We estimate it after reviewing demand, geography and offers. We do not prescribe a universal amount without those inputs.
